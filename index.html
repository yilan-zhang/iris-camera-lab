From generated form
to meaningful motion.

IRIS began with a question from a Tripo camera experiment: what makes a convincing 3D shape actually usable? This workbench brings that question to multiple objects. Explore a hybrid camera, a procedural bicycle and a robotic gripper—or bring your own segmented model and define how it moves.

This is a kinematic editor, not a physics simulator. It does not automatically segment meshes or infer joints. Built by Yilan Zhang with AI-assisted development.
