THE PROJECT

From generated shape
to something you can explore.

AI can make an object look convincing. Making that object move asks a different set of questions: where are its pivots, how do the parts connect, and what should each control actually do?

IRIS grows out of Yilan Zhang’s experiments with Tripo-generated 3D assets and interactive camera rigging. This browser edition makes that exploration visible through local-axis motion, nine articulated iris blades and a reusable part hierarchy.
